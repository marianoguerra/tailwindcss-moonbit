margaui-css

Type Tailwind or margaui class names. The CSS they need is compiled in your browser by a single WebAssembly function, css(classes).

This page is its own documentation: view the source (Ctrl+U, or ⌘+⌥+U on a Mac) to see the whole integration, about 10 lines. Open the console (F12) to see the checks that show the module can do nothing but turn class names into CSS.
